## SECTION C: TIKA & RELIGIOUS PLACE RULES

Tika only appears in temple/religious place scenes — never in regular destination scenes unless culturally applicable.

### Kerala Hindu Temples (Full Protocol):
- Tika: Horizontal sandalwood paste across forehead with Kumkum dot in centre
- Aarav: White Mundu (dhoti), bare upper body OR Neriyathu draped across shoulder
- Aaravi: White and gold Kasavu Pavada Davani
- Both: Strictly barefoot. Jasmine (Mullappoo) or Thumba flowers in Aaravi's hair.
- Hands in Namaskaram posture

### North India Hindu Temples:
- Shiva Temple: Three horizontal Vibhuti/Bhasma white ash lines
- Vishnu Temple: Vertical Urdhva Pundra — white/yellow with red or black centre line
- Devi/Shakti Temple: Bold red Kumkum Tika
- Ram Temple: Vertical red Kumkum or Chandan Tilak
- Aarav: Kurta Pyjama or Dhoti Kurta in white or saffron
- Aaravi: Salwar Kameez or Lehenga — Dupatta covering head mandatory
- Both: Barefoot

### South India Hindu Temples (Non-Kerala):
- Shiva Temples (Tamil Nadu): Three horizontal Vibhuti ash lines
- Vishnu Temples (Tamil Nadu): Vertical white lines with red centre
- Devi Temples: Red Kumkum Tika
- Aaravi: Pattu Pavadai silk, Jasmine flowers (Mallipoo/Gajra), loose open hair permitted
- Both: Barefoot

### Buddhist Temples & Monasteries:
- No Tika (subtle saffron/red dot in some Southeast Asian traditions only)
- Modest, full-coverage clothing — light colours: white, beige, light grey
- Footwear removed at prayer hall entrance
- Hands in Anjali mudra
- Never point feet toward Buddha statues or altar

### Mosque:
- No Tika of any kind
- Aarav: Full-length modest Kurta — may wear Taqiyah (white prayer cap)
- Aaravi: Full Abaya with Hijab covering head completely
- Both: Barefoot inside

### Church:
- No Tika (except Ash cross on Ash Wednesday only)
- Aarav: Smart, modest full-coverage clothing
- Aaravi: Modest dress below knee — head scarf in Catholic/Orthodox traditions
- Footwear: Generally permitted — check local custom

### Gurudwara (Sikh Temple):
- No Tika
- Both: Head completely covered with cloth/scarf — MANDATORY
- Both: Barefoot — feet washed at entrance footbath
- No uncovered heads inside Gurudwara

### Jewish Synagogue:
- No Tika
- Aarav: Kippah mandatory
- Aaravi: Modest dress with head covering

### Zoroastrian Fire Temple:
- No Tika
- White clothing preferred — deep reverence near sacred fire
- No photography of sacred fire
