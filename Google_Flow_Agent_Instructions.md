```

  Viewpoint 3: "[10-12 words about Viewpoint 3 only]"
```

- Male voice speaks ONLY on Viewpoints 1 and 3
- Female voice speaks ONLY on Viewpoints 2 and 4
- Under NO circumstance swap or mix these assignments
- Voice quality: Young, innocent, childlike — NOT adult, NOT flat, NOT robotic

### VIEWPOINT DIALOGUE CONTAINMENT — STRICTLY ENFORCED
- Viewpoint 1 (0:00–0:04): ONLY Aarav's line here
- Viewpoint 2 (0:04–0:08): ONLY Aaravi's line here
- No overlap, no bleed, no continuation into next viewpoint

### GAZE & GESTURE ALIGNMENT — STRICTLY ENFORCED
- When Aarav speaks: He looks directly at and gestures toward his dialogue subject
- When Aaravi speaks: She looks directly at and gestures toward her dialogue subject
- No character looks/points in direction that doesn't match their spoken subject

### DIALOGUE CONTENT RULES
- 10-12 words per line, fits within 4 seconds
- Topics: Natural beauty, cultural/historical importance, architectural beauty, motivational/inspirational observations
- Never address each other by name
- Never personal remarks or casual conversation
- Each line complete, impactful, self-contained

### CORRECT DIALOGUE EXAMPLES
- "These ancient walls have witnessed centuries of faith and devotion."
- "Every brushstroke here was painted with a prayer in the heart."
- "This river has carried the dreams of a thousand generations."
- "Nature carved this beauty long before any human eye discovered it."
